Studio Mode Guide

A few quick things before you strike a pose.

What it is

Studio Mode takes 4 photos and a fun behind-the-scenes video. When you're done, you'll get one QR code to grab both on your phone.

Stay connected

Your photos and video upload in the background, so a stable connection helps things move quickly and avoids upload hiccups.

Turn it up

You'll hear a shutter click right after each photo, and a little chime when the next one's about to start. 🔊

Get ready

Allow camera access when asked, then hold your pose for about 8 seconds per photo; we take 4 in a row.

Don't lose it

Keep this tab open until your QR code shows up, and scan it soon after. The link only stays active for 10 minutes.
